Executive profile

Risk leadership grounded in banking experience.

A practical, risk-based approach to protecting institutions, customers and the integrity of the financial system.

Hasan Imam is a financial crime and sanctions professional with more than 17 years of experience in international banking. He currently serves as Country Head of Sanctions at HSBC Singapore, with earlier career experience at HSBC and Citibank.

His areas of focus include sanctions risk management, AML/CTF, transaction and trade-based financial crime, customer due diligence, investigations, governance and regulatory engagement. He brings a cross-functional perspective that connects policy, operational execution, risk oversight and business realities.

He is also interested in the practical use of data analytics, automation and emerging technology to strengthen financial crime controls, improve management information and support better risk decisions.

“Effective financial crime control is not simply about rules. It is about sound judgement, clear accountability and controls that work in practice.”
